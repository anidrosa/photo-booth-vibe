Questions

Good to know

What’s the difference between Drop-Off and Hosted?

With Drop-Off Vibe, the booth is set up and ready, and guests use it on their own. With Hosted Vibe, a host stays at the booth the whole time to help guests and keep things moving.

Is there a minimum booking?

Yes, 3 hours. You can add extra hours: $50 per hour for Drop-Off Vibe and $75 per hour for Hosted Vibe.

Do you print photos?

Photos are digital only. Guests send photos to their phones right away, and every photo is saved in your live online gallery and your digital album.

How do I hold my date?

A $100 deposit when you book holds your date.

When do you need my custom album details?

At least 7 days before your event. You’ll approve the design before the party.

Where do you serve?

Rockland County, NY. If your event is just outside the county, reach out and ask.
